SPECIFIC AIMS

Closing navigation gaps in community oncology care

Patients treated across community oncology networks experience preventable delays between diagnosis, referral, and initiation of evidence-based care. This proposal will evaluate a navigation model designed with clinical teams and patient partners.

Aim 1. Determine whether structured navigation reduces time from referral to treatment initiation across participating clinics.

Aim 2. Identify implementation conditions associated with equitable reach, adoption, and sustained delivery.

The expected outcome is an evidence-grounded implementation strategy that can be adapted across resource-constrained oncology settings.
