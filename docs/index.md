## Accelerate research without surrendering judgment

Grantspace compiles the solicitation, derives a sponsor-aware outline, organizes evidence needs, and drafts proposal sections in bounded model calls that can run locally or through Claude. Clinical teams then correct assumptions, add questions and rules, request evidence-backed rewrites, and preserve every saved version. When selected, the review simulator applies the approved solicitation rubric through synthetic reviewer roles, exposes disagreements and causal threats, and turns accepted findings into revision tasks—without claiming to predict funding or replace human review.

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature"><strong>Sponsor-grounded intake</strong><p>Normalizes uploaded, linked, or pasted grant instructions into one authoritative source.</p></div>
  <div class="feature"><strong>Model-assisted first draft</strong><p>Drafts proposal sections from the grant ask while preserving human editing authority.</p></div>
  <div class="feature"><strong>Shared clinical workspace</strong><p>Keeps contributors, comments, questions, rules, tasks, and decisions with the grant.</p></div>
  <div class="feature"><strong>Immutable versions</strong><p>Every save creates history that teammates can inspect, compare, or restore safely.</p></div>
  <div class="feature"><strong>Synthetic sponsor review</strong><p>Grounds critique in the approved rubric and proposal snapshot, not invented personas.</p></div>
  <div class="feature"><strong>Reproducible publishing</strong><p>Publishes the team-approved document as formatted DOCX and PDF artifacts.</p></div>
</div>
